Questions

FAQ

Are the apps free?

Yes. Every app is free to download and use.

Windows says it protected my PC. Is that normal?

Yes. The apps are new and not yet code-signed, so SmartScreen may warn you the first time. Click More info, then Run anyway.

Why do Ninja Stats, Ninja Toolbox and Ninja Link ask for administrator?

Reading your FPS and your CPU and GPU sensors needs administrator rights in Windows, and so does letting your phone end or pause any app with Ninja Link. The other apps run normally.

Is Ninja Link safe?

Remote access is off until you switch it on. Only phones you pair (by scanning a QR code on your PC) can connect, every connection needs your PIN, wrong guesses lock it out, and your PC shows a pop-up whenever a phone connects. Nobody else, the developer included, can reach your PC.

Do I need Dojo Apps?

No. You can download any app on its own. Dojo Apps just makes installing and updating them easier.

Something isn't working. Where do I get help?

Press Support inside any app to message the developer, or join the Discord server.
